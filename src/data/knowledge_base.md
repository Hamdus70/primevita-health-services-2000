# PrimeVita Health Services Knowledge Base

## Overview
PrimeVita Health Services is a dedicated team of compassionate healthcare professionals committed to redefining home-based nursing and caregiving. Based in Alimosho, Lagos State, we bridge the gap between hospital care and home comfort.

When illness strikes or aging slows us down, we step into your home not just as healthcare providers, but as trusted partners who treat your parents, spouses, and family members with the utmost dignity and respect.

## Core Values
- **Compassion & Empathy**: We provide affectionate care.

- **Excellence & Reliability**: We go above and beyond industry standards, deploying rigorously screened, well-trained, and motivated staff.
- **Accessible Healthcare**: We believe healthcare is a basic human right and strive for affordability and coordination.

## Services
### 1. Home Nursing
Provided by Registered Nurses specially trained in domiciliary care for ongoing medical needs at home. Includes wound care, medication administration, and vital signs monitoring.

### 2. Elderly Care
Compassionate, professional assistance with daily living to support seniors maintaining independence. Helps with bathing, dressing, feeding, and daily activities.

### 3. Post-Hospital Recovery
Transitional care to bridge the gap between hospital and home, ensuring a safe and smooth recovery.

### 4. Dementia Care
Customized memory care by trained caregivers to help individuals live safely in familiar surroundings.

### 5. Physiotherapy
Expert in-home rehabilitation to help you regain physical strength, mobility, and independence safely.
